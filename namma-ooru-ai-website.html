01 · The problem

Weather, crop advice, government schemes, nearby services, today's news — all of it exists online. But for a rural or elderly Tamil speaker, the interface itself is the obstacle: small text, English menus, and typing stand between the person and the information they already have a right to. Namma Ooru AI removes that layer. You speak. It listens in Tamil. It answers in Tamil.

→

Language barrier — most public digital services default to English or formal written Tamil, not everyday spoken Tamil.

→

Interface complexity — multi-step apps and forms assume comfort with typing, navigation, and literacy in app conventions.

→

Access gap — weather, agriculture, and scheme information exists, but rarely reaches the people who need it most, in the form they need.

02 · How it works

One spoken question, eight quiet steps

From the moment a user speaks, the pipeline moves through recognition, understanding, retrieval, and response — surfacing back in under a breath.

01

Tamil voice input

The user asks a question out loud, in natural spoken Tamil — no fixed phrasing required.

02

Speech-to-text

The spoken question is converted into Tamil text for processing.

03

Tamil NLP & understanding

The system parses the sentence to understand what's actually being asked.

04

Identify user intent

The question is mapped to one of the assistant's known categories — weather, agriculture, schemes, services, or news.

05

Retrieve relevant information

The matching module is queried for the most relevant, current answer.

06

Generate a simple Tamil response

The answer is composed in plain, everyday spoken Tamil — not formal or literary phrasing.

07

Tamil text-to-speech

The response text is converted back into natural-sounding spoken Tamil.

08

Voice response

The user hears the answer — the loop closes without a single tap or typed word.
